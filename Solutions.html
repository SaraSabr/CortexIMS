CONNECTED CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Integrated Solutions for Every Stage of the Construction Lifecycle

From tender governance to asset operations, CortexIMS is designed to connect project workflows, evidence, and accountability within a more coherent information environment.

Explore connected capabilities that help teams coordinate delivery, trace decisions, and carry verified project information through the asset lifecycle.

EIGHT CONNECTED SOLUTIONS

01

Integrated Project Management

One Connected View of Project Delivery

Connect planning, progress, documentation, approvals, and issue tracking to give project teams a clearer view of delivery status.

Key capabilities
  • Monitor planned versus actual progress.
  • Track milestones, issues, and decisions.
  • Connect RFIs, submittals, and inspections.
  • Highlight exceptions requiring management attention.
  • Maintain traceability between decisions and supporting evidence.

Outcome to target: Clearer project visibility and more structured decision-making.

Key capabilities
  • Organize tender packages and scope ownership.
  • Track bidder queries and clarifications.
  • Maintain technical and commercial evaluation records.
  • Monitor outstanding approvals and decision gates.

Quality Management

Key capabilities
  • Track inspections and quality observations.

Handover & Closeout

Make Operational Readiness Visible Before Completion
