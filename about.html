About
Our purpose

Built from Construction Reality. Designed for Connected Project Management.

CortexIMS is a construction-focused digital solutions concept developed around a practical challenge: connecting information, workflows, and accountability across construction functions.

Our Story

From a Specific Site Challenge to a Connected Vision

The idea behind CortexIMS began with the search for a smarter HSE solution. This led to a broader realization: individual tools may address specific tasks, but they do not necessarily solve the fragmentation between construction functions.

CortexIMS builds on this insight to pursue a more connected approach to project information, construction workflows, quality, HSE, handover, and asset operations.

Connected by Design

Keep project information, workflows, and responsibilities connected across construction functions.

Our Vision

A Clearer View of Every Project and Every Asset

To support a more connected construction environment in which decisions, evidence, and responsibilities remain traceable throughout the asset lifecycle.

Our Mission

Connecting Construction Workflows Through Digital Intelligence

To shape practical digital solutions around construction teams' operational needs, improving information continuity and helping decision-makers identify issues that require attention.

Our Approach

Workflow-Driven Thinking

Start with actual operational challenges and the decisions teams need to make.

Lifecycle Continuity

Treat tender, construction, closeout, and operations as connected stages rather than isolated activities.

Measured Outcomes

Establish a baseline, test selected workflows, and evaluate results before expanding the scope.

Founder

Baher Youssef

CortexIMS was founded by Baher Youssef, whose background combines a decade of marine-officer experience with construction HSE leadership since 2015.

The founder's experience and the search for a more effective HSE solution helped shape the broader vision of connecting construction functions through a unified information approach.

Our Partnership Philosophy

We believe the right starting point is a real project challenge, not a generic software process. A potential developer partnership can begin with one live development, one or two priority workflows, and agreed measures for evaluating the results.
