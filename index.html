ABOUT CORTEXIMS

We Are CortexIMS

Digital Solutions for Smarter Construction Management

CortexIMS is a construction-focused digital solutions company dedicated to connecting project information, workflows, teams, quality, and Environment, Health & Safety (EHS) across modern construction environments.

Our approach focuses on improving project visibility, strengthening coordination, and maintaining information continuity from planning and construction through handover and facilities management.

We believe technology should simplify construction management, support better-informed decisions, and help teams maintain clearer oversight of project activities.

CONNECTED PROJECT LIFECYCLE

From Tender to Asset Operations

Construction projects generate critical information at every stage. CortexIMS is designed to connect these stages through structured workflows, traceable records, and greater visibility across the asset lifecycle.

01

Tender & Procurement

Organize tender packages, bidder queries, evaluations, approvals, and award readiness.

02

Pre-Construction

Structure submittals, mobilization information, baseline plans, and required approvals.

03

Construction

Connect progress monitoring, RFIs, quality, HSE, resources, and project records.

04

Testing & Commissioning

Track testing records, outstanding items, readiness status, and supporting evidence.

05

Handover & Closeout

Organize as-built drawings, O&M documents, warranties, asset records, and closure evidence.

06

Facilities Management

Support maintenance planning, work orders, warranty tracking, and asset history.
