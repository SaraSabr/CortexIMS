Construction Management Services, Connected by Design

CortexIMS brings together construction-focused digital capabilities designed to support project coordination, information control, delivery oversight, and operational readiness.

Tender & Procurement Management

Organize tender activities around clear packages, accountable owners, and traceable decisions.

  • Tender package registers and scope ownership
  • Bidder invitations and query tracking
  • Clarifications, addenda, and submission status
  • Technical and commercial evaluation records
  • Approval gates and award readiness
  • Transfer of awarded scope and commitments into project delivery

Document & Information Control

Structure project information so teams can track records, responsibilities, and workflow status.

  • Document registers and submission tracking
  • Revision and approval status
  • Requests for Information (RFIs)
  • Submittals and supporting evidence
  • Document traceability across project stages

Planning & Progress Monitoring

Connect planned activities, actual progress, milestones, and emerging exceptions for clearer project visibility.

  • Planned-versus-actual progress tracking
  • Milestone monitoring
  • Delay and exception visibility
  • Resource and workfront information
  • Project issue and decision tracking

Quality Management

Support structured quality workflows from inspection through corrective action and closure.

  • Inspection and quality records
  • Defect and non-conformance tracking
  • Corrective-action status
  • Quality closure evidence
  • Repeated defects and rework indicators

HSE Management

Support health, safety, and environmental workflows through structured tracking and accountability.

  • HSE observations and incident records
  • Corrective and preventive actions
  • Action ownership and status
  • Follow-up and closure evidence
  • HSE performance visibility

Commercial & Cost Monitoring

Bring commercial warning signals into the broader project-control picture.

  • Commercial status tracking
  • Procurement-related warning signals
  • Package-level visibility
  • Decision and exception records
  • Connections between project progress and commercial information

Detailed financial functionality should be confirmed against the actual product scope.

Testing, Commissioning & Handover

Support the transition from construction completion to a documented, operationally ready asset.

  • Testing and commissioning records
  • Snag lists and closure verification
  • As-built drawings and O&M documentation
  • Warranty and certificate tracking
  • Asset register preparation
  • Area and system readiness reviews

Asset & Facilities Management

Support continuity after handover by organizing verified asset information and maintenance records.

  • Asset registers linked to locations and systems
  • Preventive Planned Maintenance (PPM)
  • Corrective maintenance and work orders
  • Warranty and defect tracking
  • Service history and recurring issue visibility

Analytics, AI Assistance & Mobile Site Capture

Support the collection, review, and interpretation of project information.

  • Project and portfolio dashboards
  • Exception and performance visibility
  • Analytics and AI assistance as a platform capability
  • Mobile site evidence capture

Specific AI functions, integrations, and mobile capabilities should only be described as available features if implemented and verified.
